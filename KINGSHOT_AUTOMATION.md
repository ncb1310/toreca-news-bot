reference-character study illustration. Images are selected from prepared assets; no unattended
AI image generation, per-post custom illustration, or hero identity verification model is active.

Posts and Buffer sent status, public link and image are recorded in data/kingshot-state.json.
Sending/uncertain records stop further sends. No blind retry.
Disable: kingshot-config.json posting_enabled=false.
